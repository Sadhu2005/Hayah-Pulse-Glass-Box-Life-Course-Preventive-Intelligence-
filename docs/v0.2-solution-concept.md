## 1. Concept name

**Hayah-Pulse** *(Glass-Box Life-Course Preventive Intelligence)*

**Tagline:** An explainable, consent-first population health engine that converts longitudinal clinical, genomic, developmental, and environmental data into timely, culturally tailored preventive action.

---

## 2. Core idea

Instead of relying on rigid age-and-gender calendars that miss high-risk individuals or invite families too late, **Hayah-Pulse** is an explainable, consent-first population health engine that converts longitudinal clinical, genomic, developmental, and environmental data into timely, culturally tailored preventive action.

---

## 3. Ecosystem fit

Built to complement Abu Dhabi’s existing preventive ecosystem:

| System | Role in Hayah-Pulse |
| --- | --- |
| **Malaffi** | Unified health records (FHIR/HL7-compatible longitudinal EHR & labs) |
| **Newborn Genetic Screening** | Consented actionable genomic / family wellness context |
| **Well-Child pathways** | Pediatric milestones, developmental visits, recalls (0–18 yrs) |
| **IFHAS** | Comprehensive adult screening program (18+ yrs) |

Hayah-Pulse does not replace these programs. It prioritizes who needs outreach, which approved pathway fits, and how to engage with transparency and choice.

---

## 4. Three critical gaps closed

| Gap | How Hayah-Pulse addresses it |
| --- | --- |
| **Finds hidden & compounding risk early** | Detects elevated risk across family history, actionable genomic markers, sub-threshold lab trajectories, overdue developmental milestones, and neighborhood context—not just birthdays. |
| **Separates triage from clinical judgment** | Flags *who* needs outreach and *which* approved pathway fits, while giving doctors an auditable **Reason-for-Flag** breakdown rather than a black-box score. |
| **Builds family trust through radical transparency** | Gives every parent and adult a plain-language **Transparency & Choice Card** showing exactly *why* they were invited, *which* consented data was used, and flexible ways to attend (clinic, tele-consultation, or home visit). |

---

## 5. Design principles

1. **Glass-box by default** — every flag is explainable; major contributing factors are visible.
2. **Consent-first** — only authorized data is used; individuals and families see what was used and why.
3. **Life-course** — children, adults, and communities are covered through linked preventive pathways.
4. **Triage ≠ diagnosis** — population prioritization and pathway routing stay separate from clinician decision-making.
5. **Culturally tailored action** — language, channel, message, and mode of attendance are personalized.
6. **Abstain when uncertain** — sparse records trigger baseline onboarding, not overconfident high-risk alerts.
7. **Privacy by aggregation** — small cells (`n < 10`) are suppressed in public/regulator views.

---

## 6. What the prototype must prove

- Hidden/compounding risk beyond age-sex calendars.
- Auditable Reason-for-Flag for clinicians.
- Bilingual Transparency & Choice Ledger for citizens.
- Fairness dashboard with small-cell suppression.
- All demos run on synthetic data only.
